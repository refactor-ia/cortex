# Governance

## How decisions are made

Start material product, architecture, or policy decisions with an issue, then land accepted work through reviewable pull requests. During the architecture transition, the [target architecture](docs/architecture/overview.md) is authoritative for future Cortex product boundaries.

RefactorIA stewards Cortex product scope, architecture, releases, trademark and identity, security response, and maintainer appointments.

## Community roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Contributors | Participate through issues, pull requests, discussions when enabled, and other community spaces while following repository policies. |
| Maintainers | Review and maintain project work, make day-to-day technical decisions within the target architecture, and disclose conflicts of interest or recuse themselves where appropriate. |
| Community Moderators | RefactorIA maintainers designated to receive and handle confidential conduct reports under the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). |

GitHub's actual repository permissions and branch protection are the authorization authority. This document does not recreate a local owner or collaborator authorization model.

## Decision records and escalation

Use the issue-first workflow in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to provide context for material decisions. Maintainers should document significant outcomes in the relevant issue or pull request so the reasoning is reviewable.

Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and conduct reports follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Governance does not create paid support, guaranteed response times, or a mechanism to block ordinary local work.
